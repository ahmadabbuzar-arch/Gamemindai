Privacy Policy
How your data is handled.

GameMind AI is built to keep as little data as possible off your device.

  • Your chats, saved history, and theme preference are stored only in this browser's local storage — never on a server or database.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking or analytics of any kind.
  • When you send a message, image, or use a tool, the content is sent to Groq (our AI provider) only to generate a reply. It is not stored by GameMind AI afterward.
  • Clearing your chat history or your browser data removes everything — nothing else to delete elsewhere.

Questions about privacy? Email sarimforbusiness@gmail.com.
